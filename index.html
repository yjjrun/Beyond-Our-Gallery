How we score

A 100-point scorecard for creative businesses.

Scores measure business quality and future potential using filings, announcements, industry structure and business evidence. They are research tools, not price targets.

40%

Financial Strength

Balance sheet resilience, cash generation, profitability trend and funding flexibility.

20%

Market Position

Brand relevance, client concentration, sector standing and competitive durability.

25%

Growth

Order book, visitor recovery, content pipeline, international reach and margin expansion.

15%

Risk Resilience

Economic, customer, technology, regulatory and operational resilience under stress.
